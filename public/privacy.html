FractalXR privacy policy

Updated 13 November 2026

This policy covers the native FractalXR app for Meta Quest.

Data and network access

The Quest app collects no personal data and sends nothing anywhere. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or network features, and does not request the Android INTERNET permission.

Permissions

The app asks only for what a VR app needs to run. It reads the position of your head and controllers to draw the view and respond to your hands. Passthrough, if you turn it on, is drawn by the headset itself. The app never sees, captures or saves camera images.

On-headset storage

The app saves only its own settings and resume state on the headset, including the selected mode, fractals, colour theme and whether you have dismissed the controls card. It does not upload these settings or share them. Uninstalling FractalXR removes this stored data.

Contact

Ask a question or report a problem at FractalXR issues on GitHub.
